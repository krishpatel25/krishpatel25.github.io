IBM-ILLINOIS DISCOVERY ACCELERATOR · 2024–2025

1 cmTHE CHIP

Here it is — the thing I actually build

At one centimetre you are looking at the processor itself: the part that decides, billions of times a second, what to do next. I designed one that refuses to wait its turn.

FLAGSHIP PROJECT

A processor that runs ahead of itself

ECE 411 · SYSTEMVERILOG

Most processors do one thing at a time, in order, and stall whenever something isn't ready. This one looks further down the list, finds work it can do, does that instead, and quietly puts the results back in the right order before anything downstream notices. It also guesses which way each decision will go — and when it guesses wrong, it unwinds the mistake early instead of paying full price. Measured against CoreMark and a hand-written test suite.
